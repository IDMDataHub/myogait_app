# Example recording

One overground walk of a healthy adult (one of the authors, F. Fer, who
agreed to its publication), filmed side-on with a hand-held GoPro (50 fps) and
recorded simultaneously with Vicon motion capture (200 Hz) at the Institut de
Myologie, Paris. Loaded by **New assessment > Load the example recording**.

- `walk_example_sapiens.myogait.json`: Sapiens pose landmarks (no image).
- `walk_example_vicon.c3d`: the simultaneous Vicon trial (its capture volume
  covers about 2 s of the walk).

The full dataset, including the anonymised video, is the myogait example data.
